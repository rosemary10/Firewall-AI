SOCIAL ENGINEERING ATTACKS

Social engineering attacks manipulate people into revealing confidential information or performing unsafe actions.

1. Phishing

Definition: Fake emails or websites used to steal passwords, banking details and personal information.

Causes

  • Fake login pages
  • Suspicious email links
  • Urgent scam messages

How to Identify

  • Spelling mistakes
  • Unknown senders
  • Suspicious URLs

Prevention

  • Do not click unknown links
  • Enable multi-factor authentication
  • Verify sender identity

2. Spear Phishing

Definition: Targeted phishing attack aimed at specific individuals.

Causes

  • Information gathered from social media
  • Fake trusted identities

How to Identify

  • Personalized suspicious emails
  • Unexpected requests

Prevention

  • Verify requests before responding
  • Avoid sharing personal details online

3. Vishing

Definition: Voice phishing attacks through phone calls.

Causes

  • Fake customer support calls
  • Scam banking calls

How to Identify

  • Caller asks for OTP or passwords
  • Threatening language

Prevention

  • Never share OTPs
  • Disconnect suspicious calls

4. Smishing

Definition: Phishing through SMS text messages.

Causes

  • Fake delivery links
  • Prize scam messages

How to Identify

  • Unknown links in SMS
  • Urgent action requests

Prevention

  • Avoid clicking SMS links
  • Use spam filters

5. Social Engineering

Definition: Psychological manipulation to trick people into revealing confidential information.

Causes

  • Human trust exploitation
  • Fear and urgency tactics

How to Identify

  • Requests for confidential information
  • Pressure to act quickly

Prevention

  • Employee awareness training
  • Verify identities carefully
